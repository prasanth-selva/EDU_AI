Can you explain what photosynthesis is? I have a test tomorrow.

Of course! Think of photosynthesis like a plant cooking its own food. 🌿

Plants use three ingredients:

  • Sunlight (energy)
  • Water (from the soil)
  • Carbon Dioxide (from the air)

They mix these together in their leaves to create sugar (food for the plant) and oxygen (which we breathe!).
