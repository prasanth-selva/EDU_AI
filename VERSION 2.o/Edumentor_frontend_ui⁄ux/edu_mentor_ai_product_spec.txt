# Edu Mentor AI - Product Specification

## Overview
A world-class offline AI-powered education platform for rural students (LKG to 6th Standard).

## Design Philosophy
- **Modern Educational SaaS**: Professional for stakeholders, joyful for children.
- **Material 3 Foundation**: Structured hierarchy, clear focus.
- **Tactile UI**: Large targets, rounded corners (24px), soft shadows.
- **Engagement**: Animated mascot, micro-interactions, reward systems.

## Core User Journey
1. **Welcome**: Language selection and role entry.
2. **Identification**: Simple student login/profile selection.
3. **Exploration**: Dashboard for AI help, Quizzes, and Subjects.
4. **Learning**: Interactive AI chat and structured subject progress.
5. **Assessment**: Gamified quiz experience with feedback.
6. **Management**: Teacher tools for content indexing and student tracking.

## Technical Goals
- Dependency-free Vanilla JS for offline PWA performance.
- High accessibility (large fonts, clear contrast).
- Responsive layout (Sidebar on Desktop/Tablet, Bottom Nav on Mobile).
